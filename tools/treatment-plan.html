Clinical Treatment Plan Generator

Personalized, safety-checked treatment planning from your MPAI domain scores

Important: This tool is for educational, training, and clinical-reasoning support only. It is NOT a diagnostic instrument and does NOT replace assessment or treatment by a licensed clinician. If you are in crisis or are a client experiencing severe distress, please contact emergency services or a qualified professional immediately.
1. Enter MPAI Domain Scores

Scores come from the MPAI Personality Assessment. Higher scores (4-6) indicate more pronounced areas of difficulty; lower scores (0-2) indicate strengths.

2. Optional: Describe Concerns

Optional free text (e.g. from the Sentence Completion part or presenting concerns). Used for the safety screen and symptom cross-check.
